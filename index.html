Our mission

Turning aviation interest into real opportunity.

Flight Line Foundation is dedicated to reducing the financial and practical barriers that can limit access to aviation and aerospace education.

We create educational opportunities through hands-on learning, mentorship, career exploration, and access to aviation equipment, technology, and experienced professionals.

Our programs connect classroom concepts with real-world exposure to aircraft, aircraft systems, maintenance, flight operations, avionics, and modern aviation technology.

About the Foundation

A public charity focused on aviation education.

Flight Line Foundation is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity dedicated to expanding access to aviation and aerospace education through hands-on STEM learning, mentorship, career exploration, and practical exposure to aircraft and aviation technology.

The Foundation develops educational opportunities for youth, students, aspiring aviation professionals, and members of the community who may not otherwise have access to aviation experiences, equipment, or mentors.

Flight Line Foundation serves the St. Louis Metro East and greater St. Louis region.

Program areas

Learning from the hangar to the flight deck.

As a growing organization, we are building programs, partnerships, and educational resources across six connected areas of aviation and aerospace education.

01

Aviation Education and Career Exploration

Introducing participants to aviation careers, aircraft systems, flight operations, safety, and aerospace technology.

02

Hands-On Aircraft and Maintenance Education

Supervised practical learning involving aircraft, components, tools, maintenance practices, and avionics.

03

Aviation Knowledge and Flight Familiarization

Helping participants understand aircraft operations, navigation, weather, aviation safety, flight training pathways, and sound decision-making.

04

Aviation Technology and STEM

Exploring avionics, electronics, communications, simulation, data, and other technologies that help participants understand how modern aircraft and aviation systems work.

05

Mentorship and Aviation Pathways

Connecting participants with experienced aviation professionals who can help them explore education, certification, training, and career pathways.

06

Educational Equipment and Resource Access

Expanding access to aviation equipment, software, tools, simulators, and training materials that support hands-on learning and technical education.
